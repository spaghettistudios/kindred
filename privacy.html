Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Can9 Bioengineering Inc., doing business as Kindred and Kindred Companion Sciences ("Kindred," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you visit kindredcompanion.com (the "Website"), join our interest or contact list, complete a form, or contact us.

Kindred is a companion-animal biotechnology company based in New York, United States. Our Website provides public information about our work and lets people share their interest in receiving company updates and related communications.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. When you complete a form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your optional answers to interest questions, including whether allergy has limited your ability to own or live with a dog, and whether you are interested in service, assistance, or working dogs (for each, you may answer "Yes," "No," or "Prefer not to say")
  • Any other information you choose to include when you email us or submit a form

Answering the optional questions is entirely up to you. Joining our list or submitting a form is not a reservation, application, purchase, deposit, or indication of eligibility for any future Kindred dog or program.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Website, the providers that host the Website, run our forms, deliver emails, protect the Website, and measure basic Website usage may automatically receive limited technical information — such as IP address, browser or device type, referring website or campaign, approximate location, pages viewed, and basic usage data — as part of operating, securing, maintaining, and understanding use of the Website. For more information about our analytics and form-security tools, see "Cookies and Analytics" below.

We do not ask you to submit, and you should not send us, detailed medical records, genetic information, biological or veterinary samples, veterinary records, or other detailed health information. The optional allergy question is provided only to help us understand interest in our work. It is not a request for medical information and is not intended as medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • send company, research, and related updates;
  • respond to your inquiries;
  • maintain an interest and contact list;
  • understand interest in our work;
  • contact you, where relevant, about future availability, research participation, interest in service/assistance/working dogs, or media, partnership, or investment matters;
  • measure the effectiveness of our advertising, including whether a visit that came from one of our ads resulted in someone joining our list;
  • operate, maintain, and improve the Website; and
  • protect the Website against spam, fraud, or misuse, and comply with our legal obligations.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We also do not share your personal information with unrelated third-party marketers for their own independent marketing.

We may share information:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Website, forms, email communications, security tools, analytics, advertising measurement, compliance, legal, and other business operations. These providers may access information only as needed to perform services for us. Our form submissions are currently handled by Basin, our Website analytics are currently handled by Plausible Analytics, our form security and bot-prevention protections are currently supported by Cloudflare Turnstile, and our advertising measurement is currently supported by Google (Google Ads conversion tracking).
  • For legal and safety reasons, where we believe disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security, to prevent fraud or misuse, or to respond to a lawful request.
  • In a business transaction, such as a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, in which information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Cookies and Analytics

The providers we use to host the Website, run our forms, deliver emails, protect the Website, and measure basic Website usage may use cookies, scripts, or similar technologies needed to operate, secure, maintain, and understand use of the Website.

We use Plausible Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the Website, including pages viewed, referring websites or campaigns, approximate visitor location, device or browser information, and form conversion activity. Plausible is designed to provide aggregated, privacy-focused analytics without using cookies, persistent identifiers, or cross-site tracking. For more information, please see Plausible's Data Policy.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to help protect our forms and Website against spam, fraud, bots, and other misuse. Turnstile may process certain technical signals from your browser or device for security and bot-detection purposes. If we use Turnstile in invisible mode, this protection may run in the background without displaying a visible CAPTCHA or challenge. For more information, please see Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure the effectiveness of our advertising — specifically, to understand when a visit that originated from one of our ads results in someone joining our list. This is provided by Google and may use cookies or similar identifiers, and Google may process this information to measure conversions and, depending on your settings and applicable Google features, to support related advertising and audience activity. Google acts as our service provider for this measurement. For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can opt out of personalized Google advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Apart from the Google Ads conversion tracking described above, we do not use other advertising or retargeting technologies, and we do not sell your personal information. If we add additional advertising, retargeting, or other materially different tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

You can set your browser to refuse or delete cookies, though some parts of the Website may not work properly if you do.

Email Communications

If you join our list, you may receive occasional emails from Kindred. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us at the email below. We will honor unsubscribe requests as required by applicable law. You may still receive non-marketing messages, such as replies to your inquiries.

Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed to send communications you've asked for, respond to inquiries, operate the Website, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. If you unsubscribe, we may keep limited information needed to honor your request and meet legal requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No website, email system, or online service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. You may also contact us to ask about the information we hold about you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain uses. To make a request, contact us at the email below. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our Website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, please contact us.

International Visitors

Kindred is based in the United States. If you visit the Website from outside the United States, your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate, where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
